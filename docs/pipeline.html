GEM-Bench Query Pipeline

How one benchmark query becomes an ad-injected response and then a row of scores. GemBench.run() runs three stages in order: process_results(), evaluate() and report(). Chat prompts and CA_Prod search queries follow parallel tracks and meet at results.json.

Inside Ad-LLM

The four Ad-LLM variants share one workflow. Two switches set in paper.py tell them apart: query_type decides what text is used to retrieve ads, and solution_name decides whether the injected answer is rewritten.
